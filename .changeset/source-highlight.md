The Markdown mode is highlighted: headings, emphasis, links, code, lists and quotes in colour (the kit's tokenizer over `@dskripchenko/syntax`), by a copy of the text over the field — the field's caret, selection and undo stay the browser's, and a host that styles the field keeps its style. Past 300 000 characters the source stays plain.
